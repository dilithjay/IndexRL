Few-shot Multispectral Segmentation with Reinforcement Learning

Authors: Dilith Jayakody, Thanuja Ambegoda

Published at: BMVC 2024

Abstract

The task of segmentation of multispectral images, which are images with numerous channels or bands, each capturing a specific range of wavelengths of electromagnetic radiation, has been previously explored in contexts with large amounts of labeled data. However, these models tend not to generalize well to datasets of smaller size. In this paper, we propose a novel approach for improving few-shot segmentation performance on multispectral images using reinforcement learning to generate representations. These representations are generated as mathematical expressions between channels and are tailored to the specific class being segmented. Our methodology involves training an agent to identify the most informative expressions using a small dataset, which can include as few as a single labeled sample, updating the dataset using these expressions, and then using the updated dataset to perform segmentation. Due to the limited length of the expressions, the model receives useful representations without any added risk of overfitting. We evaluate the effectiveness of our approach on samples of several multispectral datasets and demonstrate its effectiveness in boosting the performance of segmentation algorithms in few-shot contexts. The code is available at https://github.com/dilithjay/IndexRLSeg.

Introduction

Multispectral images (MSIs) are extensively used in diverse applications such as environmental monitoring, crop analysis, and tumor detection. Each MSI captures information in multiple spectral bands, allowing for the extraction of unique spectral signatures. However, collecting large-scale annotated MSI datasets is expensive and time-consuming. This limits the applicability of deep learning-based segmentation methods in real-world settings. In this paper, we explore the few-shot setting where only a handful of labeled samples are available, and present IndexRL – a method that learns mathematical expressions using reinforcement learning to combine MSI bands effectively for segmentation.

Methodology

IndexRL formulates the task of discovering effective spectral band combinations as a reinforcement learning (RL) problem. The agent generates index expressions consisting of arithmetic operations over spectral bands (e.g., (B5 − B4)/(B5 + B4)), which are then evaluated based on their contribution to segmentation performance. The RL agent is trained using a reward signal derived from the segmentation accuracy on a validation set. This process avoids exhaustive search and allows efficient discovery of meaningful band combinations without supervision.

Results

We conduct experiments on three benchmark MSI datasets: Pavia University, Salinas, and Indian Pines. IndexRL consistently outperforms traditional index-based and deep learning-based baselines in few-shot settings. The discovered expressions generalize across scenes and datasets, demonstrating the robustness of our method. Quantitative metrics such as mIoU and overall accuracy show a significant improvement over baselines, confirming the efficacy of reinforcement learning for spectral index discovery.
